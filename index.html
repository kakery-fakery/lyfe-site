Lyfe

The chores and groceries of one household, on two phones.

Lyfe is an iPhone app for the two people who run a home. It keeps one shared record of what needs doing and when, who did it, what the shop needs and the recipes you cook from, and shows the same thing on both phones.

Chores repeat on their own schedule and sort themselves into today, this week and later. Ticking one off records who did it and when; pushing one back keeps the record honest. The grocery list sorts itself by aisle and takes a whole pasted list at once, and a recipe's ingredients go straight onto it.

Support Privacy policy
Lyfe is made by Kyle Baker. It collects only what it needs to work and never tracks you; the privacy policy says exactly what that is.
