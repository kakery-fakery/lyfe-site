Lyfe is an iPhone app for the two people who run a home: one shared record of chores, groceries and recipes, the same on both phones. It needs an internet connection; what you see is what the server last said, and pulling down on a list refreshes it.

Getting started

Tap Create account, type your email and a password (at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number), and type the six-digit code from the email Lyfe sends you. Then Start a household and pick your name and colour, or Join with a code if the other person already has one.

Common questions

The code hasn't arrived

Look in your junk or spam folder; the email comes from Lyfe. It can take a few minutes. If it still hasn't come, tap Send a new code, and check the address you typed.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?. A code goes to your email; the code and a new password sign you in.

How does the other person join?

On your phone, the Household tab shows the invite code, with Share next to it. On their phone, after creating their account, they tap Join with a code and type it. Both Household tabs then list two members. If a code gets out, New code on the Household tab stops the old one working.

Reminders aren't showing

Reminders are on the Household tab under Remind me: on days something is due or overdue, the phone says what, at the time you set. If Lyfe isn't allowed to notify you, turn notifications on in Settings › Lyfe › Notifications.

The phones show different things

Pull down on the list to refresh. Both phones read the same record, so once each has refreshed they agree. If a change was refused with "Already updated on another phone", the other person got there first and the list shows what they did.

Leaving a household, or deleting my account

Both are on the Household tab. Leave household keeps your account; the household keeps its record and what you ticked off stays in your name, and you can join again with the code. Delete account leaves the household first and then removes your account and sign-in for good. What Lyfe keeps and for how long is in the privacy policy.

Something else

If the app does something it shouldn't, email the address above with what you did, what happened and what you expected. The iOS version (Settings › General › About) helps.
