Lyfe Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

In short
  • Lyfe collects your email address, the name and colour you pick, and what you put in the app: chores, groceries, recipes and who did what. Nothing else.
  • It uses them only to run the app. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no selling or sharing with anyone outside your household.
  • Everything is stored on Amazon Web Services in the United States and deleted when you delete your account, which you can do in the app at any time.

Who runs Lyfe

Lyfe is made and run by Kyle Baker, an independent developer. For anything about your data, email support@example.com.

What Lyfe collects

WhatWhy
Email address It is your sign-in name. Lyfe emails you a code to confirm it when you create the account and when you reset your password. It is linked to your account.
Name and colour The display name and colour you choose. They mark what you tick off, so the other person in your household can see who did what. Linked to your account.
What you put in the app Your household's name, its chores with their schedules and notes, each completion, postponement or skip with who did it and when, the grocery list and the recipes. Shared with the members of your household and no one else.

That is all. Lyfe does not collect your contacts, location, photos, calendar, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage analytics or crash reports. It has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software. Lyfe does not track you across other apps or websites, and its App Store privacy label says the same.

How it is used

To run Lyfe: to sign you in, to show the household's record on both phones, and to send the confirmation and password-reset codes. Your data is not sold, rented or used for advertising, and it is not shared with anyone except the service providers below, who process it only to provide the service.

Where it is kept

Lyfe's accounts and record live on Amazon Web Services in the US East (N. Virginia) region, in an account controlled by the developer: sign-in and accounts in Amazon Cognito, the record in Amazon DynamoDB, and the emailed codes sent through Amazon Simple Email Service. The connection between the app and these services is encrypted (HTTPS), and the data is encrypted at rest. Backups are kept for up to 35 days, after which deleted data is gone from them too.

On your iPhone

  • Your sign-in token is stored in the iOS Keychain so you stay signed in. It is removed when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Your reminder setting (on or off, and the time) and a note of which pattern notices this phone has already shown are stored in the app's own settings on the phone.
  • Reminders are notifications the phone schedules for itself. Lyfe uses no push notification service, so nothing about reminders leaves your phone.

Your household

Lyfe is for the people of one home. Anyone who joins your household with its invite code sees everything in it: the chores, the list, the recipes, and your name and colour on everything you tick off, with the time. Make a new invite code from the Household tab if one gets out.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Your account and what it holds are kept for as long as the account exists. To delete your account, open the Household tab and tap Delete account: you leave the household first and your account and sign-in are then removed at once. The household's shared record (its chores, list, recipes and past completions, including those in your name) stays for the person still in it. If you were the last member and want the household's record removed too, email the address above from your account's email address. You can also just Leave household, which keeps your account.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: everything Lyfe holds about you is on screen in the app; your name and colour are changed on the Household tab.
  • Delete: in the app, as above, at any time and without asking anyone.
  • A copy: email the address above from your account's email address and you will get what Lyfe holds about you.

Children

Lyfe is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If a child has made an account, email the address above and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date.
